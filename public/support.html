Frequently asked questions

Can I buy food from this preview?

This site currently demonstrates the shopping experience. Sample products and demo orders are not real purchases. Check the checkout notice before proceeding; live sales require confirmed product information, merchant setup and published purchase policies.

Where can I find ingredients and feeding guidance?

Look for verified information on the product page and the actual product label. Sample descriptions are not verified nutritional advice. Ask your veterinarian about your pet’s individual dietary needs.

How much will delivery cost?

Delivery areas, charges and delivery estimates must be confirmed in the shipping policy and checkout before a real purchase. Demo shipping amounts are illustrative.

Can I return pet food?

Check the published returns policy before purchasing. Rules for opened food, damaged items and refunds have not yet been confirmed for this preview.

Why does my basket stay after I refresh?

The storefront saves product identifiers and quantities in this browser’s local storage. You can remove items from the basket or clear site data in your browser settings.
